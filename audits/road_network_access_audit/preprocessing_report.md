## 5. Pedestrian access rules and ambiguous cases

Classification uses only the `access` and `foot` tags (trimmed, case-insensitive; empty = missing). `highway` and `highway_category` play no part. Priority:

1. **Restricted**: `access` or `foot` is one of no, private, use_sidepath, regardless of the other tag.
2. **Conditional**: no restrictive value, and either tag is one of permissive, destination, customers, permit, forestry, emergency, tidal.
3. **Walkable**: everything else, including features where both `access` and `foot` are missing or empty.

> **Working assumption:** features with both `access` and `foot` missing are treated as Walkable. This is a working assumption, **not verified evidence** of pedestrian access: absence of a tag does not confirm that pedestrians are permitted. The previous highway-based Conditional fallback for service and major roads has been removed. Treat the 'both missing' count below as the unverified portion of Walkable.
